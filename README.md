> **Definition**

> **Information Job Shop (IJS)** is a computational organization paradigm in which information itself becomes the workpiece processed, routed, verified, assembled, and manufactured by heterogeneous computational resources.

---

## Why This Repository?

For more than seventy years, computing has been organized around the Von Neumann paradigm:

> **Instructions operate on data.**

This abstraction transformed the modern world.

However, recent developments in:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI Agents
- Tool Calling
- Multi-Agent Systems
- Human-AI Collaboration
- Brain Unit Architectures
- Runtime Planning

suggest that another computational abstraction is gradually emerging.

The primary computational workpiece is no longer simply data.

It is

> **Information itself.**

This repository introduces **Information Job Shop (IJS)** as an engineering framework for understanding this transition.

---

# The Central Idea

Traditional manufacturing organizes production around **Jobs**.

A Job carries a workpiece through different machines according to its required processing route.

Information Job Shop proposes a remarkably small—but fundamentally important—change.

Instead of processing physical workpieces,

future computational systems increasingly process

> **Information Jobs.**

Examples include:

- software development
- engineering design
- scientific discovery
- planning
- reasoning
- education
- legal analysis
- creative work

The workpiece changes.

Everything else naturally follows.

# Why It Matters

Modern AI systems already exhibit many characteristics of Information Job Shops.

Information Jobs continuously:

- generate new prompts,
- create subtasks,
- invoke tools,
- retrieve knowledge,
- collaborate with humans,
- verify intermediate results,
- modify their own execution routes.

Unlike traditional manufacturing,

Information Jobs are active computational entities.

They continuously participate in their own manufacturing process.

---

# Information Job Shop Is Not Another AI Algorithm

This repository does **not** introduce:

- a new LLM,
- a new neural network,
- a new reasoning algorithm,
- a new programming language.

Instead,

it proposes a higher-level engineering organization for future computational systems.

Just as Job Shops transformed industrial manufacturing,

Information Job Shops may transform computational manufacturing.

This repository does **not** attempt to complete the theory of Information Job Shops.
