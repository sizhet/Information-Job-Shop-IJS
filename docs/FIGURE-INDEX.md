# New in Version 1.0.1 — Information Manufacturing Evolution

Version 1.0.1 extends the Information Job Shop (IJS) framework beyond Information Manufacturing by introducing **Autonomous Information Job Shop Evolution (AIJSE)**.

The two figures below summarize the evolutionary direction of Information Manufacturing and provide the conceptual bridge from the original IJS architecture toward future autonomous intelligent systems.

---

## Fig-006 — Autonomous Information Job Shop Evolution (AIJSE)

**Purpose**

Illustrates the complete closed-loop evolution architecture of Information Manufacturing.

Unlike traditional manufacturing systems, Information Job Shops continuously improve themselves through feedback from their own information products.

The figure demonstrates how information artifacts may participate in future computation, update knowledge structures, modify processors, influence runtime Scheduler/Dispatcher decisions, generate new workflows, construct new Information Job Shops, and ultimately improve the capability of future information manufacturing.

**Key Concepts**

- Active Information Pieces
- Runtime Feedback Loops
- Knowledge Evolution
- Scheduler/Dispatcher Evolution
- Processor Evolution
- Workflow Evolution
- Information Job Shop Creation
- Continuous Structural Evolution
- Autonomous Information Manufacturing

**Primary Message**

> Information Manufacturing continuously improves its own capability to manufacture future information.

---

## Fig-007 — Evolution Levels of Information Manufacturing

**Purpose**

Presents a layered roadmap describing how Information Manufacturing evolves from basic information processing toward fully autonomous information manufacturing ecosystems.

Instead of viewing computation as isolated information processing, the figure organizes Information Manufacturing into progressively more capable structural levels.

**Evolution Levels**

- Level 0 — Process Information
- Level 1 — Generate Information
- Level 2 — Generate Knowledge
- Level 3 — Generate Processors
- Level 4 — Generate Information Job Shops
- Level 5 — Generate Better Information Job Shops

**Primary Message**

The long-term evolution of Information Manufacturing is not centered on producing increasingly more information.

Instead, each generation improves the capability of manufacturing future information.
