# Fig-001

## From Physical Jobs to Information Jobs

Associated document:

> **001 — Why Information Becomes the Job**

Purpose

Introduces the fundamental paradigm shift.

The Industrial Age manufactures physical jobs.

The AI Age increasingly manufactures information jobs.

Core idea

> **Information becomes the computational workpiece.**
